ADVERSE EVENT REPORTING

If you become aware of a suspected side effect, it should be reported. Reporting forms and information can be found at www.mhra.gov.uk/yellowcard or search for MHRA Yellow Card in the Google Play or Apple App store.

Adverse events should also be reported to the Medical Information Department at Syri Limited by Telephone: 0208 515 3700 or
Email: medinfo@syrimed.co.uk.

This information contributes to the continued safety surveillance of our products.
