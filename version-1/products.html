LICENSED FORMULATIONS

SyriMed develops, licences and markets liquid medicines for oral use.

SyriMed develops, licences and markets liquid medicines for oral use. These liquid medicines can be formulated as solutions, syrups and suspensions. In May 2020 SyriMed held over 50 marketing authorisations for medicines in the UK. SyriMed is constantly developing and licensing new formulations of liquid medicines which meet the unmet medical needs of patients such as developing a medicine that has not been previously available in an oral liquid formulation so as to make administration easy for paediatric patients and those who have swallowing difficulties.
