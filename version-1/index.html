Who we are

Value Added Medicines, formulated around patients

Syri Limited trading as SyriMed is a UK-based research and development company. We specialise in improving generic molecules by formulating advanced proprietary formulations adapted to the clinical needs of patients — improving their care and ease of administration.

  • Precise dosing for conditions such as epilepsy, where exact dosing and dose tapering are crucial
  • Liquid medicines for patients unable to swallow tablets, including paediatric patients and those with dysphagia
  • Tablets in new strengths for accurate dosing and easier administration

Pharmacovigilance

Report a suspected side effect

If you become aware of a suspected side effect, it should be reported. Reporting forms and information can be found at the MHRA Yellow Card website, or search for MHRA Yellow Card in the Google Play or Apple App Store. Adverse events should also be reported to our Medical Information Department.
