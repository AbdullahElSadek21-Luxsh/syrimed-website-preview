Formulation Development

SyriMed has a well established formulation development team. The team consists of well qualified and experienced formulation research scientists who work to develop novel formulations of established molecules.

Our formulation development strategy is driven by innovative science, strong scientific understanding and the clinical need of the patient.

Whilst developing our medicines we emphasise the concept of Quality by Design with the aim of developing stable and well accepted products for the intended patients.
